Raja's Tomb (Gaddige) – Royal Tombs of Kodagu

Gaddige houses the Indo-Saracenic domed royal tombs of King Doddaveerarajendra and Lingarajendra with intricate brass carvings and hillside panoramas.

Historic Monument

Visitor Information & Highlights

Raja's Tomb, locally called Gaddige, features three striking Muhammadan-style domed structures with minarets honoring Kodagu rulers. The central tomb belongs to King Doddaveerarajendra and his queen, while the right tomb honors Lingarajendra.

Situated on an elevated ridge about 2.5 km from The Town Villa Coorg, Gaddige offers quiet heritage surroundings and picturesque views of Madikeri's gentle hills.
